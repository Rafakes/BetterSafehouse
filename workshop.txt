description=[b]Overview[/b]
description=Better Safehouse improves safehouse management in [b]Project Zomboid Build 42[/b], with a focus on multiplayer quality of life, admin tools, and optional custom claiming.
description=
description=Supports:
description=
description=* [b]B42.15[/b]
description=* [b]B42.16[/b]
description=* [b]B42.17[/b]
description=* [b]B42.18[/b]
description=* [b]B42.19[/b]
description=
description=[hr]
description=
description=[b]Main Features[/b]
description=
description=* Blue overlay to view the safehouse area
description=* Enhanced safehouse invites
description=* Optional multiple safehouse membership as a member
description=* Admin tools for safehouse management
description=* Optional custom safehouse claiming
description=* Optional PhunZones 2 integration
description=* Extra Sandbox controls for vanilla and custom safehouses
description=* Better Safehouse management tab integrated into the vanilla Safehouse menu
description=* Optional force respawn at the selected safehouse
description=* Safehouse expansion system with configurable permissions for players and server roles
description=
description=[hr]
description=
description=[b]Custom Claim Options[/b]
description=The mod includes optional custom safehouse claiming with Sandbox settings.
description=
description=Available options:
description=
description=* Item-based custom claim
description=* Free custom claim without item
description=* Custom safehouse size
description=* Claim location restrictions
description=* Custom-only servers with vanilla safehouse disabled
description=* Multi-direction safehouse expansion (if allowed by server permissions)
description=
description=[hr]
description=
description=[b]Admin Features[/b]
description=Admins can:
description=
description=* Add players to safehouses more freely
description=* Release/remove safehouses without changing the owner first
description=* Manage safehouses with more flexibility in multiplayer servers
description=* Configure expansion permissions for players and roles
description=
description=[hr]
description=

description=
